[h1]Scatterspam Capture[/h1]

[b]Load your Scatter Sphere Launcher—because it was never really 0%.[/b]

Tired of tiny capture chances getting rounded away? This mod shows rates below
1% with three decimals, so 0.006% stays 0.006%.

It also hides the message that floods your screen during sphere spam:

[quote]This Sphere isn't working on [Pal name][/quote]

Your actual capture chance stays unchanged.

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS
Experimental (Palworld)[/url].
