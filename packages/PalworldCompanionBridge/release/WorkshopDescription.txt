[h1]Palworld Companion Bridge (Experimental)[/h1]

Connects Palworld to local AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude through the PalCom companion server.

Use [b]Hey PalCom,[/b] in chat for private answers, inspect your bases and Pals, and access experimental allowlisted management tools. Communication stays on your machine and opens no network port.

Requires UE4SS Experimental and the local [url=https://github.com/dienp/palworld-mods]Palworld MCP/PalCom server[/url].
